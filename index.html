About me

The first thing I learned about reliability had nothing to do with servers. A bank transfer from Ghana to Europe ran late, and a shipment for my business, Blisstrade, sat at the port while demurrage charges grew each day. I worked with my bank and my agent, and I asked the shipping line to release the cargo before the money had cleared. They agreed because years of honest dealing had earned their trust. I collected the goods on time and paid no demurrage.

That is the kind of engineer I want to be: someone who finds the problem, talks to the right people and follows through. In 2024 I finished a seven-month DevOps bootcamp with JOMACS International. I am now studying for a bachelor’s degree in Information Technology at BYU-Pathway Worldwide. I live and work between Ghana and Italy.

Projects

Each project lists what I did, what was hard, and what it taught me.

Deploying an application with Docker Compose

Docker, Docker Compose, Linux, YAML, networking

I packaged an application and its supporting services into containers so they run the same way every time. I set up the Compose file with services, ports, environment settings and volumes, and worked through the errors until everything ran.

What was hard and what I learned (click to open)

A small mistake in a configuration file can stop a whole application, and the cause is rarely where the error first appears. I learned to check one service at a time, read the logs first, and confirm how services connect before changing anything. Changing several things at once makes it impossible to know what helped.

AWS infrastructure with Terraform (team project)

AWS, Terraform, Linux, cloud networking

Our team built the AWS setup for an application, all defined as code: a VPC, public and private subnets, EC2 instances, security groups, an Application Load Balancer, Amazon RDS and Route 53. I helped build and configure it in Terraform and worked out how each piece connects.

What was hard and what I learned (click to open)

Network settings, security rules, database access and DNS all have to be right at the same time. When something failed, we traced the connections and reviewed the Terraform plan before applying any change. Working in a team taught me to ask questions early and listen, and the plan step taught me to know what a change will do before I make it.

Reverse proxy and database setup

Nginx Proxy Manager, Docker Compose, PostgreSQL, Linux

I put Nginx Proxy Manager in front of an application and a PostgreSQL database so web requests reach the app and the app reaches its data. I configured the proxy, connected the services and fixed the configuration problems along the way.

What was hard and what I learned (click to open)

Ports, hostnames, network settings and database details all have to line up. When a connection failed, any of the three could be the cause, so I learned to test each link in the chain one at a time.

A delayed shipment at Blisstrade (business case study)

Negotiation, communication with banks, agents and shipping lines

A late international bank transfer put a shipment at risk of demurrage charges. I worked with my bank and agent to speed up the payment and asked the shipping line for a telex release before the funds cleared. The cargo arrived on time and I paid no demurrage. I now start international payments earlier and leave room for banking delays.
